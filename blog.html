When Is the Best Time for a Maasai Mara Safari?

The Maasai Mara is wonderful all year round, but the experience changes with the seasons. Here’s how to choose.

July to October: the Great Migration

This is the Mara’s most famous season. Huge herds of wildebeest and zebra arrive from Tanzania’s Serengeti, and the dramatic Mara River crossings draw predators close. It’s also the busiest and most expensive time, so book early.

January to March: quieter and great value

The weather is usually warm and dry, the crowds are smaller and rates are lower. The resident wildlife — lions, elephants, cheetahs and leopards — is still easy to spot.

April, May and November: the green seasons

Rain falls mostly in short afternoon showers. The landscape turns lush and green, lodges offer their best prices and you may have sightings all to yourself.

Our tip

If the migration is your dream, aim for August or September. If you want value and fewer vehicles, consider January to March. Either way, combine your safari with a few days on the coast to relax afterwards.

Greek Island Hopping: How to Choose Your Islands

Greece has more than 200 inhabited islands, which makes choosing the hardest part. Most first-time visitors start with the Cyclades, a group of islands linked by regular ferries from Athens.

Santorini for views and romance

Clifftop villages, caldera sunsets and volcanic beaches. Perfect for couples and honeymoons.

Mykonos for beaches and nightlife

Lively beach clubs, a pretty old town with windmills and plenty of energy after dark.

Naxos and Paros for a relaxed pace

Long sandy beaches, mountain villages and excellent food at lower prices — great for families.

Our tip

Two or three islands in a week is plenty. Travel in May, June, September or early October for warm sea, fewer crowds and better prices than in July and August.

Thailand for First-Timers: What to Know Before You Go

Thailand is one of the easiest and most rewarding places to travel in Asia. A classic first trip combines a city, the north and the islands.

A simple first route

  • Bangkok — the Grand Palace, Wat Pho, floating markets and street food.
  • Chiang Mai — the old city, mountain temples and ethical elephant sanctuaries.
  • Phuket or Krabi — beaches, island-hopping boat trips and the famous limestone cliffs.

When to go

November to April is the driest and most popular time for the Andaman coast, including Phuket and Krabi. The rest of the year is greener and quieter, with showers that usually pass quickly.

Local customs

Cover your shoulders and knees when visiting temples, take off your shoes before entering, and always speak respectfully about the Thai royal family.
