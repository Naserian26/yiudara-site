Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a trip?

Choose a destination in the search box above, or tap the WhatsApp button, and tell us your dates and group size. We’ll send you a tailored itinerary and quote, and confirm your booking once you’re happy with it.

Can you customise a tour for me?

Yes — every trip we plan is tailor-made. Tell us your budget, interests and travel style and we’ll build the itinerary around you.

Do you help with visas and travel documents?

We’ll let you know what documents your destination requires and guide you through the process before you travel.

Do you organise group and family trips?

Absolutely. We plan trips for couples, families, friends and larger groups, including corporate and celebration travel.

How can I pay?

Payment options and deposit details are shared with your quote. Get in touch on WhatsApp or by email if you have any questions.
