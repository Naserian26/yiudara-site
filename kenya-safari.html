Featured Tour

Kenya’s Best Safari & Beach Experience

Wildlife, adventure and coastal luxury — all in one unforgettable journey.

Duration8 days / 7 nights
StartsNairobi
StyleSafari + Beach
GroupPrivate or small group

Overview

This trip brings together the two sides of Kenya everyone dreams about. First, the open plains of the Maasai Mara, home to the Big Five and, from July to October, the Great Migration. Then the palm-fringed beaches of Diani on the Indian Ocean, where you can slow down and simply enjoy the sun.

The itinerary below is a sample — we can add nights, switch to Amboseli or Lake Nakuru, or choose a different beach to suit you.

Sample Itinerary

  1. Day 1

    Arrive in Nairobi

    We meet you at the airport and transfer you to your hotel. If time allows, visit the Giraffe Centre or the elephant orphanage.

  2. Day 2

    Into the Maasai Mara

    Drive west past the Great Rift Valley viewpoint to the Maasai Mara, arriving in time for your first afternoon game drive.

  3. Day 3

    Full day in the Mara

    Morning and afternoon game drives in search of lions, elephants, leopards and more. Optional sunrise hot-air balloon ride and Maasai village visit.

  4. Day 4

    Fly to the coast

    A final early game drive, then fly to the south coast and settle into your beach resort in Diani.

  5. Days 5–7

    Diani Beach

    Relax on the white sand, snorkel with dolphins at Kisite-Mpunguti Marine Park, sail on a traditional dhow or visit the Shimba Hills.

  6. Day 8

    Departure

    Transfer to the airport for your flight home, with memories of both the bush and the beach.
